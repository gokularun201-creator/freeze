Freeze AGI

Privacy Policy | Package: com.freeze.assistant | Effective: September 12, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Freeze AGI ("we", "our", or "the App") is built from the ground up with a strict privacy-by-design architecture. Freeze AGI is designed to act as an autonomous, hands-free personal voice and automation assistant.

  • No User Account Required: Freeze AGI does not require registration, login credentials, or account creation.
  • No Remote Profiling: We do not operate remote user profile databases or log your personal usage habits.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or automation routines.

2. Android AccessibilityService API Prominent Disclosure

Mandatory Play Store Disclosure: Freeze AGI utilizes the Android AccessibilityService API solely to perform user-requested touch interactions and navigations on your behalf.

Specific Purpose: When you issue explicit spoken voice commands or trigger quick console actions (e.g. "Click Subscribe", "Scroll down", "Like this video", "Accept cookies", "Go back"), Freeze AGI translates your intent into assistive screen gestures.

  • Zero Credential Monitoring: Freeze AGI NEVER captures, reads, logs, or transmits passwords, financial records, credit card numbers, or private chat communications.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Operation: UI tree element inspection is executed transiently in volatile device RAM and discarded immediately upon task completion.
  • Revocable Access: You can enable or disable the Accessibility Service at any moment via Android Settings > Accessibility > Freeze AGI.

3. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Freeze AGI requires access to the microphone strictly to transcribe your spoken voice instructions and detect the hands-free wake word.

  • Offline Wake-Word Detection: Hands-free wake word spotting ("Hey Freeze") is handled 100% locally and offline on your device using a bundled Kaldi acoustic model. Audio is not transmitted to cloud servers while waiting for wake prompts.
  • Visual Indicators: Whenever the microphone is active, both the Android OS system indicator and the on-screen glowing Voice Orb illuminate clearly.

4. Google Gemini AI Integration (Bring Your Own Key)

For complex conversational knowledge queries, Freeze AGI connects directly to Google's official Gemini API endpoints using your personal API key (Bring Your Own Key model).

  • Queries are transmitted over direct, end-to-end encrypted TLS 1.3 connections directly to Google.
  • No intermediary proxy servers intercept or inspect your conversational prompts.
  • Your API key is saved locally in private app storage on your device.

5. Telephony & Contacts Permissions

Access to READ_PHONE_STATE and READ_CONTACTS is used on-demand exclusively for:

  • Identifying and announcing incoming callers hands-free.
  • Voice dialing when you instruct the assistant: "Call [Contact]".
  • Call audio is never recorded, and your contact book is never uploaded to remote servers.

6. Floating Assistant Bubble & Foreground Services

The SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission enables the compact floating Voice Orb on top of other applications. A persistent system notification is displayed while foreground services run, ensuring complete visibility and providing an instant 1-tap termination control.

7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because all configuration data and history reside strictly on your local device hardware:

  • You can completely wipe all local settings and history at any time by going to: Android Settings > Apps > Freeze AGI > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling Freeze AGI permanently and irreversibly deletes 100% of stored app files and settings from your device.
